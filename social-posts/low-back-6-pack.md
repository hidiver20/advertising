## Notes to maximize conversion

1. **Link straight to checkout.** The direct Acuity link drops people into the 6-Pack purchase with one tap. A short, memorable redirect on your website (for example, TherapeuticConnection.com/6pack pointing to the Acuity link) would look cleaner in posts and on flyers. That redirect does not exist yet; you would set it up in your website settings.
2. **The posts are written to be boost-safe.** Meta's ad rules prohibit copy that "asserts or implies personal attributes," including "physical or mental health (including medical conditions)." Their own examples: "Do you have diabetes?" is prohibited, while "New diabetes treatment available" is allowed. These posts describe the offer and the condition without saying the reader has back pain. If you edit them, avoid hooks like "Is your back killing you?" or "Suffering from back pain?"
3. **Your Acuity checkout already has a Meta Pixel installed** (it appears in the page code). If you boost these posts, you can track 6-Pack purchases as conversions and retarget people who clicked but did not buy.
4. **Add a real deadline only if one exists.** Urgency lifts conversion. If you decide to cap the offer (for example, "price good through December 31" or "first 10 buyers"), add that line above the 👉 link. The posts make no scarcity claims as written.
5. **Collect a low back testimonial.** The written testimonials on your site cover other concerns, so the posts quote them as general praise. One short quote from a 6-Pack graduate about their low back would strengthen every post. Your testimonials page also lists a video titled "Have back pain and headaches? Physical Therapy with Therapeutic Connection can help you!", which would make a strong Reel to share during the series.
6. **Years of experience.** Your website says "35+ years," while your style guide and Substack say "30+." The posts use 35+, matching the website. Pick one figure for all materials.
7. **Kūpuna version.** Acuity also has "6 Pack for Seniors: Say Good-bye to Back Pain" at $500, and its description lists the value as $750. Any post can be adapted for an older audience by swapping in that link and price. The $750 value differs from the $900 you would get by multiplying six sessions at $150, so check that figure before advertising it.
8. **Optional disclaimer.** If you want the educational disclaimer from your handouts on social posts, a short version fits in a comment or at the end: "For educational purposes only. Not a substitute for individualized medical advice."
