## Text: Take Control: Protect Yourself from Falls

*Carolyn Silan, PT* (text copied verbatim from Canva page 2)

1. **The stakes are real** - Falls significantly increase mortality risk, and hip fractures are especially dangerous: up to 30% of people who fracture their hip die within one year.
2. **How you move matters** - Changes in your walking pattern or posture are red flags for fall risk.
3. **Surprising culprits** - Even diabetes, depression and autoimmune diseases increase fall risk through frozen shoulder and neuropathy. Managing these conditions protects your stability.
4. **Medication alert** - Taking more than 5 medications significantly raises your fall risk. A medication review could be your best safety step.
5. **The #1 predictor: previous falls** - Fallen in the last year? Your risk of falling again is significantly higher. Talk to Carolyn for personalized guidance - don't wait.
6. **Use it or lose it** - Physical inactivity leads straight to muscle weakness and balance problems, creating a dangerous cycle.
7. **Simple hazards like loose rugs or poor lighting cause preventable falls!!** Go to my website for a Home Safety Checklist at https://therapeuticconnection.com/events.
