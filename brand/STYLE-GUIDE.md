## Typography

- Headlines: heavy geometric sans serif (Poppins-style), bold and large, often underlined on seminar flyers
- Body: same sans family in regular weight
- Checklists use ☐ boxes; closing reminders use 🌀; section headers are numbered ("1. Brush & Balance")

## Layout patterns

**Handouts / lead magnets (8.5 x 11 in portrait)**
1. Header row: logo, title, headshot
2. Thin gray rule under the header
3. Numbered or checkbox content, with simple line-drawing illustrations or Carolyn's own exercise photos
4. A "Want 1-on-1 Support?" or "Already in Pain?" call to action pointing to TherapeuticConnection.com/schedule
5. Educational disclaimer in small italics
6. Standard four-item footer with icons: 📞 808-289-0601 · 🌐 TherapeuticConnection.com · ▶ VIDEOS: YouTube.com/@therapeuticconnection · ARTICLES: TherapeuticConnection.Substack.com
7. Two-page pieces end page 1 with "CONTINUED ON PAGE 2 →"

**Seminar flyers (8.5 x 11 in portrait, community bulletin boards)**
1. Logo, topic image, headshot across the top
2. Underlined bold title, often a question or a hook ("Is Housecleaning a pain in your ____?")
3. Date and time in red: "FREE SEMINAR + Post-Seminar PAU HANA Gathering!"
4. Venue: WVA Ohana Room or Community Room, "(to the right of Johnny's Restaurant)"
5. 2 to 4 short punchy lines of copy
6. Tear-off tabs along the bottom, rotated 90°, with topic, date, 289-0601 and TherapeuticConnection.com
7. "RSVP 'Going' on FB" and a QR code on some versions

## Voice

- Warm, neighborly, and a little playful ("EAT EARLY! Yup, even if it labels you OLD!", "Unknot Yourself," "talk story," "🤙")
- Myth-busting hooks: "Most of what you've been told about stretching is WRONG," "Falling Asleep Instantly is NOT a Good Sign!"
- Direct second person, short sentences, exclamation points, occasional ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Local Hawaiʻi touches: Pau Hana, ʻohana, 🌺
- Offers are framed as free with a "$10 suggested donation"
- Lead magnet CTAs are specific and benefit-first: "FREE 20-Minute Steady Balance Class," "Complimentary 3-minute balance assessment," coupon code "Balance"
